The UV mapping was reused from the engine's existing fragPixelScaling.glsl,
whose embedded source notice identifies the author, source URL and CC0 license.
Mosslume's mosslumeWind.glsl adapts it for local moving-sprite coverage using
four NEAREST texel-center samples and premultiplied-alpha interpolation.
The original notice is retained in the derived shader. No global texture
filtering or full-screen blur is introduced by this adaptation.
